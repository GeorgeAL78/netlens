### Added
- Device page: Wi-Fi history, every connect, disconnect and roam over 24 hours, 7 or 30 days, roams shown from where to where with band, channel and signal on each side, moves UniFi did not log taken from NetLens's own samples, and the time spent on each access point. Also for scripts and assistants as `get_device_wifi_history`.
- Traffic UniFi cannot name ("Unidentified") says where it went, from the device's connection records: e.g. a Ring doorbell's video "Went to amazon.com 99%", and its sessions read "Unidentified · likely amazon.com" when one owner has most of it.
